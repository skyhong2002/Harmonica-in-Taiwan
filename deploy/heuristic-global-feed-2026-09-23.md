# Global English visitor heuristic evaluation — 2026-09-23

Scope: home stories, public Google Calendar, and the unified three-column post river. Real Chromium evaluation of https://harmonica.observe.tw/ and /post/, then source inspection and targeted fixes. This is an expert heuristic review using regional scenarios, not interviews with representative users.

Scenarios: an English-reading US visitor in America/Los_Angeles at 1440×900; an English-reading UK visitor in Europe/London at 390×844; browse international posts, find US sources, compare calendar times, filter calendars, recover from no matches, start following, and expand original text. Retained the original three-column document-scroll layout and the real Google Calendar embed.
