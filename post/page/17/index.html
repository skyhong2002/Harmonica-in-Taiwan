【2026 Asia Pacific Harmonica Festival Day 1】
The 15th Asia Pacific Harmonica Festival was held last week in Jiangyin, Wuxi. Harmonica enthusiasts and performers from different regions gathered together to exchange, share, and celebrate their love for harmonica music!
On the first day, we presented an ensemble lecture titled “From Basics to Perfection: An Introduction to Harmonica Ensemble Playing.” We introduced three different types of harmonicas and shared insights into common challenges encountered during ensemble practice, engaging in discussions with everyone. Thank you to the audience for their enthusiastic questions, and special thanks to the two groups of children who bravely came on stage to perform and share their questions. We hope this lecture helped everyone gain a better understanding of harmonica ensemble playing. 🤍
That same evening, we also performed at the opening concert held at the local Grand Theatre.…
