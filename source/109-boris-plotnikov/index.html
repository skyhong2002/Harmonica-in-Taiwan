Boris Plotnikov plays "Georgia On My Mind" on the JDR Trochilus in Bb.
Some songs just seem to ask for the Trochilus.
Could I play this on a diatonic harmonica? Absolutely. But following the chord changes faithfully requires a lot of overblows. That's why many diatonic players simplify the melody, leaving out some of the beautiful notes that highlight the harmony.
Could I play it on a chromatic? Of course. But for a bluesy song like this, the chromatic still has a slightly more academic sound.
For me, the Trochilus is the perfect fit.
