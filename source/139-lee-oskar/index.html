A big thank you to @leeoskarharmonicas Lee Oskar Harmonicas for making me an official Harmonica Advocate, and for the LowRider harmonica that came with it.
The Advocate program recognises people who have committed themselves to the instrument: performers, com… A big thank you to @leeoskarharmonicas Lee Oskar Harmonicas for making me an official Harmonica Advocate, and for the LowRider harmonica that came with it.
The Advocate program recognises people who have committed themselves to the instrument: performers, composers, teachers and content creators, regardless of which brand they play.
In their words: “If they’re moving the music forward and spreading their passion for harmonica, that’s enough for us.”
Eight advocates are featured worldwide at the moment, and I am the one in Germany.
It is an honour to be recognised by a company that has accompanied players around the world for decades, and to be trusted to represent the way I teach: harmonica and guitar at the same time, one person, learned in the right order.
The LowRider is their low tuned harp. It drops a full octave, so the tone comes out deeper and warmer under a guitar.
What is the first song you would want to hear on a low, warm harmonica?
