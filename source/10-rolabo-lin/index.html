The second volume of my tremolo harmonica solo music score book, Push-Pull Arpeggios, is now in its final check stage. I would like to take this chance to share the tracklist and the keys used with you.
Following the style of my previous work, Tongue Killer (which used fewer harmonicas in its later part), these new songs focus on testing the limits of playing a single harmonica. Because of this, not many keys are required. I truly hope this collection reaches and inspires more tremolo harmonica players out there!
