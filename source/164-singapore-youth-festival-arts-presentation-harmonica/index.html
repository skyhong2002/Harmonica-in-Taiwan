Introducing SYF 2026 Art Exhibition: Artist and XPerience 🌟
The exhibition theme invites Secondary and Pre-University students to explore their personal artistic growth and the transformative power of the creative process — from breakthrough moments and happy… Introducing SYF 2026 Art Exhibition: Artist and XPerience 🌟
The exhibition theme invites Secondary and Pre-University students to explore their personal artistic growth and the transformative power of the creative process — from breakthrough moments and happy accidents, to the evolution of their unique creative voices. 🎨✨
Bringing this theme to life, students from Victoria School, Bukit View Secondary School and the Japanese School, Singapore came together for Cross-Cultural Inquiry Through Art, a pre-exhibition fringe activity held at the SMU public space. Through urban sketching and collaboration, students from different school communities and cultural backgrounds connected through art.
Their works will be showcased alongside other student artworks at the SYF 2026 Art Exhibition, held at National Gallery Singapore from 4–19 July 2026.
Join us in celebrating the creative journeys of our young artists! 🧑‍🎨
