@yenhua_wang : 個人很喜歡和弦口琴的存在，它可以做出很有簧片風味的節奏和和聲。可惜認真吹這種樂器的人很少。很少人吹的原因，我認為其中之二是演奏這個樂器本身需要足夠的耐心和熱情（它挑人），加上市場相對小眾（比演奏旋律的口琴小）。但是，和弦口琴是口琴重奏的特色，甚至可以說，如果沒有它，我覺得口琴重奏就沒有什麼特別的了———個人想法但就是這種感覺！

We dearly miss Eloy Porto Neto, whose deeply expressive trombone playing shaped the soul of this recording and left an… Three years ago, we recorded this beautiful Taiwanese song, “Your Name Engraved Herein”, composed by Kaoru Wada and made famous by Crowd Lu.
We dearly miss Eloy Porto Neto, whose deeply expressive trombone playing shaped the soul of this recording and left an indelible mark on all who heard him. His artistry, generosity, and spirit continue to resonate far beyond this moment. Though Eloy passed away in 2024, his legacy lives on in every note we share. A colaboration between Brazilian and Taiwanese musicians.
Andre Grella- keyboards
Edu Helene - flute
