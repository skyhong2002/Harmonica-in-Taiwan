Yip Chun Hei is a Hong Kong Chromatic Harmonica player and a core member of RedBricks, a well-known harmonica ensemble, with a passion for musical performance and teaching.
Yip has been actively competing in both international and local events, earning numerous awards and honors. He won Adult Duet Championship and Adult Orchestra Championship at the World Harmonica Festival in Germany, and also secured Third place in the Chromatic Harmonica Solo with Test Piece Category. At the Asia-Pacific Harmonica Festival, he won Champion in the Open Category for Chromatic Harmonica Solo. He also became the youngest finalist at the Hong Kong International Chromatic Harmonica Competition, where he earned Third place. Yip has been serving as a judge for the Seoul International Harmonica Festival and the Asia-Pacific Harmonica Festival for consecutive years.
