On Friday, two duos presented their new albums:
Hermine Deurloo + Anton Goudsmit opened with their soulful music full of joy and passion. (Album release: May 15, 2026)
Their concert was followed by the powerful blues of Keith Dunn and Klaas Wendling who brought the music of their new album (release May 29, 2026) on stage.
The 3rd concert of the evening was dedicated to old time and traditional playing styles performed by US player Seth Shumate. extra: Seth not only invited fiddle player Ryland Burhans. He also ask Adam Sikora to come on stage.
Naturally, the jaw harp – played by Sören Birke – was a must at this concert. It was the first time different free reed instruments had come together at this festival – with more to follow.
