Harmonica warm up #2!
This harmonica warm up features a single note scale motion in a repetitive pattern. Doing this really opens up the breathing, single note stability and pitch control. Do it maximum for 1 minute sessions, maybe 3-5 sessions as a warm up. Take it easy and slow first - work up the speed and don't rush it.
We are using the first five notes of the major scale, hole 4-6 on diatonic and holes 5-7 on chromatic harmonica. You can do either the eight note version or 16th note version.
On my Patreon you will get the PDF for free.
Best,
Filip

Diatonic players:
Use a G diatonic - pattern DDBB DDBB then DDDDBB DDDDBB

Metronome on 80 bpm all the time.

How to use chord theory to map out your harmonica improvisations:
In this video, I break down how I navigate between tension (the dominant) and resolution (the tonic) to create paths to walk while improvising. I'm playing freely in the key of D major using a C chromatic 14-hole harmonica, calling out the chords as I go (Root, 5-chord, 6-minor, 4-chord) so you can hear how they feel inside the major scale and get a picture of what is going in my thoughts when I play.
I'm practicing outdoors today because on the the best things about the harmonica is that you can always just take it out into nature and play. Go outside and play music!
