I’ve played Toronto many times over the years, and the city has always held a special pla… Toronto! I’m thrilled to be bringing The Howard Levy 4 to @therextoronto! Join us for two nights—July 17 & 18 at 10:30 PM—for the band’s first-ever Toronto performances.
I’ve played Toronto many times over the years, and the city has always held a special place in my heart. I still remember hunting for hard-to-find LPs at the legendary Sam the Record Man on Yonge Street!
Toronto also has an incredible jazz legacy. As a teenager, hearing Jazz at Massey Hall with Charlie Parker and Dizzy Gillespie completely changed my life. That recording opened up a whole new world for me, and it’s a thrill to be returning to a city with such deep jazz roots.
I can’t wait to share the stage with this amazing band and spread some bright moments at The Rex.
See you July 17 & 18 at 10:30 PM!

This is a very personal album for me: twelve solo pieces i… Exciting news, my friends—the first physical copies of my new solo album, Heavy World, are here, and I’ve already had the joy of handing some of them to people in person after our show a few days ago.
This is a very personal album for me: twelve solo pieces in just about every style, including originals, improvisations, and a few unexpected interpretations. Among them are my definitive version—and first studio recording—of “Amazing Grace,” and “Niccolò’s Notion,” the very first perpetual-motion piece written for diatonic harmonica, inspired by Paganini’s famous “Moto Perpetuo.”
Putting this album together has been a deep and demanding experience—pushing me to my limits on piano, harmonica, percussion, and more.
And in a time when so much music and art arrives instantly, digitally, and often disappears just as quickly, I found myself wanting to do something a little different—something more tangible, more personal, and maybe a bit old-fashioned in the best sense.
So there will be no streaming release just yet, and no instant digital…

Howard Levy 4 is a dynamic, multi-styled band hailing from Chicago, led by Grammy-Award winning diatonic harmonica master, pianist and composer Howard Levy. Joining him are the incredibly virtuosic guitarist Chris Siebold, the outstanding upright and electric bassist Joshua Ramos, and the masterful Brazilian drummer Luiz Ewerling.
This powerhouse ensemble delivers pure musical magic in a range of styles that includes Jazz, Latin, World Music, and Blues. Besides several distinctively reworked American and Brazilian tunes, the band has an astonishingly diverse repertoire of original compositions that combine and blend many cultures, rhythms and genres, creating a sonic experience that's nothing short of extraordinary.
