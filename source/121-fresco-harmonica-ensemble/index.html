🎵 Horse Racing 《赛马》 | Opening Concert – 15th Asia Pacific Harmonica Festival 2026
FRESCO Harmonica Ensemble was honoured to collaborate with the Jiangyin Liu Tianhua National Orchestra for a special performance of the iconic Chinese composition “Horse Racing” (赛马) at the Opening Concert of the 15th Asia Pacific Harmonica Festival.
Bringing together the expressive sound of harmonica and the powerful colours of a traditional Chinese orchestra, this performance was a memorable musical collaboration that celebrated the beauty of music and the spirit of cultural exchange.
We are grateful for the opportunity to share the stage with the talented musicians of the Jiangyin Liu Tianhua National Orchestra, and to be part of this unforgettable opening concert.
🎼 Performance: Horse Racing 《赛马》
