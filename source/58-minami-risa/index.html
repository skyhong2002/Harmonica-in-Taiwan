Today’s song is “Shiki no Uta” (Song of the Four Seasons), written and composed by Toyohisa Araki.I performed it on the chromatic harmonica.
This beautiful Japanese song expresses both the beauty of Japan’s four seasons and the warmth and kindness of the human heart.
It was selected as one of the “100 Best Japanese Songs” and continues to be cherished by generations of listeners.
