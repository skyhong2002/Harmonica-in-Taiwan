What an honor it is to teach this special seminar at the largest harmonica convention in the United States!
This is where it all began for me. In 1998, at the age of 19, I came here as a young musician, full of dreams and passion for this small — yet incredibly powerful — instrument.
Today, so many years later, I have the incredible joy of being back here, not only as a musician, but also helping and inspiring a new generation of harmonica players and, above all, helping to spread and celebrate our small but great instrument: the harmonica!
Life takes us on amazing journeys. And being able to return to the place where I once started, now sharing what I have learned throughout all these years, is an honor that words can hardly express.

During the 12th Seoul International Harmonica Festival, I had the honor of being invited by the festival’s organizing committee to give a special performance at the pre-festival welcome dinner.
It was a truly meaningful moment for me. For many years, I had dreamed of playing Celtic music accompanied by an orchestra. Since this was a solo performance, I found a way to bring that dream to life by performing over an orchestral backing track.
The piece I chose was Countess Cathleen, a masterpiece composed by Bill Whelan and made famous through @riverdance_official. It is one of the most beautiful and moving melodies I have ever heard, and I had always hoped to perform it on a special occasion.
I can’t think of a better setting than a festival that brings together so many extraordinary musicians and dear friends from around the world.
I hope this performance moves you as deeply as this music has always moved me.
Here is the full performance.
