🎵 Phrygian Harmonica Lesson – Modal Playing on Harmonica
In this video, we explore the sound of Phrygian on harmonica.
Phrygian has one of the darkest and most recognizable modal colors – mainly because of the flat 2nd. That note immediately gives the mode its tense, exotic, slightly Spanish sound.
But this lesson is not about running another scale up and down.
It’s about hearing the mode, feeling the atmosphere, and learning how to turn the Phrygian color into musical ideas on the harmonica.
Inside the video, I demonstrate the Phrygian sound and show how different soundscapes can bring out different sides of the mode.
