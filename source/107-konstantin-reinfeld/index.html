🎵 The Chicken Harmonica Lesson – 11th Position on a C Harp
In this video, we explore The Chicken in B♭ on a C harmonica.
The obvious choice would be an E♭ harmonica in 2nd position. Instead, we use 11th position – a surprisingly bluesy and practical way to approach the tune.
This arrangement was inspired by our encore with the Sirius Harmonica Ensemble from Taiwan at the Seoul International Harmonica Festival.
The progression includes D7, G7 and C7 – the same dominant chords we already know from playing a regular 12-bar blues in G on a C harp. That makes several parts of the tune feel familiar and gives us a strong foundation for improvisation.

🎵 Mixolydian Harmonica Lesson – G Mixolydian in 2nd Position
In this video, we explore G Mixolydian on a C harmonica.
That means 2nd position – the position where the blues is practically built into the harmonica.
The characteristic note is the flat 7th. Together with the major 3rd, it creates the open, earthy and slightly bluesy Mixolydian sound.
We play the scale over a G cello drone and improvise over 4 original soundscapes.

🎵 Lydian Harmonica Lesson – Modal Playing on Harmonica
In this video, we explore the sound of the Lydian mode on harmonica.
Lydian has one of the brightest, most open and most recognizable modal colors – mainly because of the raised 4th. That single note gives the mode its floating, spacious and slightly magical character.
But this lesson is not about running another scale up and down.
It’s about hearing the mode, feeling the atmosphere, and learning how to turn the Lydian color into musical ideas on the harmonica.
Inside the video, I demonstrate F Lydian over an F drone, use the Lost Woods Theme from The Legend of Zelda as a familiar musical example, and introduce a rhythmic F–G chord pendulum to improvise over.
